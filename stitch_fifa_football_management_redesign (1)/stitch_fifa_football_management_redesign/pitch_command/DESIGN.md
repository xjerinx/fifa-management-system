## Brand & Style

This design system delivers an ultra-premium, operational command environment tailored for elite football governing bodies, tournament directors, match delegates, and technical technical staff. The aesthetic balances television broadcast graphics precision with mission-critical tactical intelligence. 

The visual tone is authoritative, hyper-focused, and athletic:
- **Tone & Mood:** High-fidelity, analytical, pressurized yet composed, institutional yet technologically forward.
- **Design Style:** High-Density Broadcast & Technical Dark System. Deep obsidian and midnight pitch tones layered with micro-contrast hairline borders, razor-sharp tabular typography, vibrant match event accents (pitch emerald, tournament cyan/electric blue, and card amber), and calibrated data density.
- **Audience:** Tournament directors, match commissioners, referee coordinators, technical analysts, and stadium operation commanders requiring split-second legibility across real-time match data, roster sheets, and disciplinary workflows.

## Colors

The color palette reflects elite stadium environments under floodlights, pairing deep architectural slate with broadcast-grade semantic accents:

### Surfaces & Neutrals
- **Canvas Base (`#0B0F17`):** The master ground plane for all operational workspaces.
- **Surface Layer 1 (`#111827`):** Primary structural shells, side navigation, and top bars.
- **Surface Layer 2 (`#1A2234`):** Cards, data tables, module panels, and segmented control containers.
- **Surface Layer 3 (`#1F2A3F`):** Hover states, active row states, and secondary floating panels.
- **Hairline Borders (`#26334A`):** Low-contrast structural dividers (1px) maintaining visual discipline without visual noise.

### Broadcast & Operational Accents
- **Pitch Emerald (`#10B981` / Active: `#059669`):** Primary brand accent denoting live match clocks, confirmed player substitutions, approved match sheets, and pitch readiness.
- **Tournament Cyan & Electric Blue (`#38BDF8` / `#2563EB`):** Secondary accent for competition governance, UEFA/FIFA institutional badges, VAR verification status, and tactical telemetry.
- **Match Event Amber (`#F59E0B`):** Tertiary status for caution alerts, disciplinary yellow cards, stadium capacity thresholds (85%+), and pending operational checks.
- **Disciplinary Scarlet (`#EF4444`):** Direct red cards, critical facility failures, pitch delays, and medical alerts.

### Typography Colors
- **Text Primary (`#F8FAFC`):** Display scores, player surnames, timer counters, and primary metrics.
- **Text Secondary (`#E2E8F0`):** Standard data values, table cells, and navigation labels.
- **Text Muted (`#94A3B8`):** Micro-labels, stadium metadata, timestamp watermarks, and column headers.

## Typography

Typography establishes an instant operational tempo. Three dedicated typefaces serve distinct roles across the platform:

1. **Display & Section Headers (`Chivo`):** High-energy, sharp, condensed-leaning geometric grotesque with sports broadcasting DNA. Used for match scores, venue names, competition titles, and uppercase structural tabs.
2. **Body & Dense Information (`Hanken Grotesk`):** Contemporary, neutral, and razor-sharp across varying monitor resolutions. Powers general UI copy, operational workflows, delegate notes, and table content.
3. **Telemetry & Timestamps (`JetBrains Mono`):** Fixed-pitch numerals guarantee absolute vertical alignment during high-frequency updates across match stopwatches (`90+4'`, `ET 118'`), coordinate tracking, kit numbers, and tabular stadium turnstile counts.

### Rules of Usage
- All match times, coordinates, technical statistics, and seat numbers must use `JetBrains Mono` with tabular figure variants enabled (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`).
- Table column headers and tactical badges utilize `label-caps` in uppercase with explicit tracking (`0.08em`).
- Player names follow continental football convention: Initial lowercase first name with all-caps surname (`K. DE BRUYNE`).

## Layout & Spacing

The platform uses a 12-column adaptive grid designed for high-density command dashboards and multi-panel operational views.

### Screen Adaptations
- **Desktop Command (`1440px` and above):** 12-column structure with fixed 72px collapsed or 240px expanded global left rail. Main workspaces support 3-column splits (e.g., Live Pitch Graphic [col 1–6], Real-time Event Stream [col 7–9], Delegated Actions [col 10–12]). Gutters are locked to `16px` to maximize screen real estate.
- **Tablet Operational (`768px - 1439px`):** 8-column layout. Multi-panel modules convert into stacked split-views with horizontal carousel tabs for pitch tactical views and bench rosters.
- **Mobile Handheld Field Kit (`< 768px`):** Single-column stacked stream. Complex tables collapse into stacked cards showing key status, live clock, score, and tap-to-expand details.

### Spacing Philosophy
Spacing operates on a strict 4px base increment. Data tables use compact vertical padding (`space-xs` / 8px) to allow up to 25 visible rows per viewport height without vertical scrolling. Margin and padding across all widgets are purposefully compact to eliminate empty visual gaps.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system rejects ambient drop shadows in favor of a structural architectural tier system defined by surface tonal shifts and sharp hairline borders.

### Surface Tiers
- **Tier 0 (Ground - `#0B0F17`):** Underlay canvas visible only in application margins and background viewport areas.
- **Tier 1 (Panels & Shell - `#111827`):** Primary layout containers, headers, and docking drawers, outlined with a 1px solid border in `#26334A`.
- **Tier 2 (Interactive Modules & Cards - `#1A2234`):** Cards, individual match fixtures, roster slots, and table row groupings.
- **Tier 3 (Active Overlays & Menus - `#1F2A3F`):** Tooltips, position detail popovers, dropdown action menus, and modal dialogs. Features a high-contrast border of `rgba(56, 189, 248, 0.25)` or `rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.35)` when linked to active operations.

### Glow & Telemetry Focus
Ambient lighting is restricted exclusively to active operational indicators:
- **Live Match Signal:** Emerald point indicator with a 4px blur halo (`box-shadow: 0 0 8px rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.6)`).
- **Critical Incident / VAR Under Review:** Electric blue pulse halo (`box-shadow: 0 0 12px rgba(56, 189, 248, 0.4)`).

## Shapes

The design system employs a disciplined, angular, and functional shape language (`roundedness: 1`). Radii are kept small and deliberate to maintain a professional, instrumentation-grade feel.

- **Standard Elements (Cards, Panels, Inputs, Buttons):** `4px` (`0.25rem`). Gives an intentional, machined aesthetic reminiscent of stadium perimeter boards and tactical control boards.
- **Position Badges & Tag Modules:** `2px` (`0.125rem`). Sharp micro-rectangles that preserve space within dense tabular rows.
- **Player Avatars & Kit Roundels:** Circular (`50%` / `9999px`) to create clear geometric contrast against angular data tables.
- **Borders:** Consistent `1px` crisp strokes using `#26334A`, avoiding decorative corner bevels or heavy outlines.

## Components

### 1. Buttons
- **Primary Operational:** Pitch Emerald background (`#10B981`), Text `#0B0F17` (bold), 4px radius, `font-family: Chivo`, uppercase, `12px` label with subtle letter-spacing. Hover: `#059669`.
- **Secondary / Action:** `#1F2A3F` background, 1px border `#26334A`, text `#F8FAFC`. Hover: Border `#38BDF8`, text `#38BDF8`.
- **Destructive / Red Card:** `#EF4444` background with white text, or ghost variant with 1px `#EF4444` outline and `#EF4444` text.
- **Sizing:** Compact (`28px` height for table actions), Standard (`36px` height for toolbar actions).

### 2. Micro-Badges for Player Positions & Tactical Roles
- Compact 22px × 18px rectangular blocks with `label-data` JetBrains Mono typography:
  - **Goalkeeper (GK):** Amber background (`rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.15)`), text `#F59E0B`, border `1px solid rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.3)`.
  - **Defender (DF / CB / LB / RB):** Blue background (`rgba(56, 189, 248, 0.15)`), text `#38BDF8`, border `1px solid rgba(56, 189, 248, 0.3)`.
  - **Midfielder (MF / CM / CDM / CAM):** Emerald background (`rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.15)`), text `#10B981`, border `1px solid rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.3)`.
  - **Forward (FW / ST / LW / RW):** Rose/Red background (`rgba(244, 63, 94, 0.15)`), text `#F43F5E`, border `1px solid rgba(244, 63, 94, 0.3)`.

### 3. Live Match & Telemetry Indicators
- **Live Match Pill:** Dark container with a pulsing emerald micro-dot (`6px`), bold text "LIVE", followed by a tabular stopwatch (e.g., `74:12`).
- **VAR Flag Badge:** High-visibility cyan tag (`#38BDF8` text, black background, 1px cyan border) paired with an interrogation icon for pending reviews.
- **Disciplinary Cards:** Crisp 10px × 14px vertical rectangular shapes representing Yellow (`#F59E0B`) and Red (`#EF4444`) with 1px dark borders.

### 4. Tabular Data & Scannability
- **Header Row:** Surface `#111827`, border-bottom `1px solid #26334A`, text uppercase `Chivo` at `11px`, color `#94A3B8`.
- **Data Rows:** Background `#1A2234`, alternating row shade `#161E2E`, hover highlight `#1F2A3F`. Cell height locked to `36px` (compact) or `44px` (standard).
- **Numeric Alignment:** All goals, cards, distances, and capacities must align right using `JetBrains Mono`.

### 5. Competition Tabs & Navigation Rails
- Segmented control tabs constructed on `#111827` surface with 2px inner padding. Active tab sits on `#1A2234` with an emerald indicator bar (`2px` height) pinned to the top or bottom edge.
- Tab items display competition badges alongside match count indicators (e.g., `GROUP STAGE (8)`).

### 6. Stadium Capacity & Incident Meters
- Progress bars: 4px track height, surface `#111827`, filled with `#10B981` (under 80%), transition to `#F59E0B` (80-95%), and `#EF4444` (capacity breach / alert status).

### 7. Form Controls & Filters
- Input fields use `#111827` background, 1px `#26334A` border, `13px` Hanken Grotesk text. Focus state triggers a clean 1px border highlight in `#38BDF8` with zero outer shadow blur.
- Dropdowns feature integrated position/status chips directly inside the selection token.
